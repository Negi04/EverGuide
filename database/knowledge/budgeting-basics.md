## What a budget is for

A budget is a plan for how you will use your money each month. It helps you cover essentials, avoid unnecessary debt, and make progress towards goals. A budget is not about never spending on things you enjoy; it is about deciding in advance what matters most to you.

## Start by tracking

For one month, record all income and spending, using a notebook, a spreadsheet or a banking app. Group spending into categories such as housing, food, transport, phone and internet, education, debt payments, savings, and personal spending. Tracking first shows where money actually goes, which is often different from what people expect.

## A simple starting framework

One commonly used guideline is the 50/30/20 approach: about half of take-home income for needs, about a third for wants, and about a fifth for saving or paying down debt. These proportions are a starting point, not a rule. In high-cost cities or on a first salary, needs may take a larger share, and that is normal.

## Pay yourself first

Many people find it easier to save when a fixed amount moves to savings automatically at the start of the month, rather than saving whatever is left at the end. Even a small amount builds the habit.

## Review and adjust

Review your budget each month. If you regularly overspend in one category, either adjust the plan or look for a specific change, such as cooking more often.
